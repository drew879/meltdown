## A. Bold ink cartoon

Key art for the title screen of a mobile word game called MELTDOWN, portrait 9:16. Scene: a suburban backyard on the first warm day of early spring. The snow is thawing in patches and wet green grass shows through. A wooden fence runs along the back, with a bare tree showing its first buds, a few purple and yellow crocuses pushing up through the snow, and a birdhouse with dripping icicles. In the lower middle stands a dapper snowman made of three stacked snowballs, wearing a small bowler hat and a striped knitted scarf, with a carrot nose, coal eyes, coal buttons and twig arms. He is a ham putting on a brave face: a nervous grin, one bead of sweat, eyes glancing sideways up at the sun, standing in a small puddle of his own meltwater. In the upper right is the sun with a face: smug, half-lidded eyes and a self-satisfied smirk, very pleased with himself. Across the top third is the single word MELTDOWN in big, chunky custom lettering, spelled exactly M-E-L-T-D-O-W-N, with the bottom edges of the letters just starting to drip. No other text anywhere. Keep the bottom fifth of the picture calm (grass and snow) so buttons can sit there later. Clear focal order: title, snowman, sun. Funny, warm and playful, never sad or scary. The snowman is an original design and must not resemble any existing snowman character from film or television.

STYLE: bold ink cartoon. Thick, confident outlines of even weight in deep indigo, not black. Flat, saturated colour with one step of cel shading. A limited palette of about seven colours: snow white, cool blue shadow, fresh spring green, sky blue, warm sun yellow, a coral red accent and the indigo ink. Crisp, clean edges like premium vinyl sticker art or a modern editorial cartoon. Art-directed, professional game key art. No gradients, no airbrushing, no glossy plastic shine, no 3D render look, no lens flare, no clutter, no extra characters, no watermark, no signature, no interface buttons.
