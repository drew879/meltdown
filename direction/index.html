Meltdown

The pitch

Melvin is a very dapper snowman who booked the wrong holiday. He packed for the slopes and landed on the hottest beach in the world.

Ray is the sun, and Ray thinks this is the funniest thing he has ever seen.

Every wrong letter turns up the heat. Guess the word before Melvin is a puddle.

Palette

One small set of colours for the art and the buttons alike.

Type

Bagel Fat One

For titles, scores and big moments. It is soft and round, like something made of snow.

Nunito, for everything you read

Clear, friendly letters. The keys and the puzzle tiles both use it, so the letter you tap looks the same as the letter that appears.

Both are open-licence fonts (OFL) served from the game itself.

How it is drawn

Melvin and Ray are drawn by code on a canvas, sixty times a second, from a handful of numbers such as "how melted" and "how worried". That means any difficulty can stop at any point in the melt, every guess can get its own reaction, the lines stay sharp on any screen, and all of the art weighs less than one photo. The buttons, tiles and keyboard are ordinary web elements styled to match, so they work with VoiceOver.

How it will sound

Each piece of music starts as MIDI written by a script, with a rough preview so we can settle the tune in chat. Then it is rebuilt in Ableton Live with better instruments and exported as layers. The game stacks those layers as the heat rises, so the music builds without ever skipping a beat. Sound effects are made in code.
