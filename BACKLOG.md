# Meltdown: feature backlog

Ideas and feedback to pick up later. Not a plan: nothing here is scheduled or approved. When one is built, move it to "Done" with the date. Decisions that change the brief still go in `BRIEF.md`.

Each item says where it came from and, where relevant, which phase it likely belongs to.

## Ideas

### Snowman reacts to a correct guess (Phase 2, animation)

When a letter is guessed correctly, the snowman makes a fun little positive gesture, chosen at random from a handful. Examples: a fist pump, a smile, a wink. The gesture should fit his current melt stage (a fist pump needs an arm; a puddle can only manage something small). Come up with a handful and show Drew the list before building. Needs new poses per stage, or a reduced set for the late stages.

### Repeated letters fill in one after another (Phase 2, animation)

When a guessed letter appears more than once in the answer, the copies appear in sequence, quickly (a short stagger left to right), not all at once.

### Answer colours: underscores and letters (visual polish)

The blanks and the revealed letters are both white, which is boring and off-theme. Give them slightly different colours from each other, and make both look more natural against the wooden sign (for example chalk, paint or carved tones, rather than flat white). Show a few options before settling.

### Keyboard keys: a more on-theme look (visual polish)

Keep the rough paper background on each key. Change the letter itself to a more natural-looking font and style, and let the keys sit a little less perfectly aligned (small offsets or tilts). Not so much that the keys look scrambled or unbalanced, and tap targets must stay the same size and easy to hit. Show a few options before settling.

### Player names and scores: separate them clearly (visual polish)

The top bar currently reads like "SAM 2" and "DAD 1", and it is not obvious that the name is the player and the number is the score. One idea: lay a second piece of paper over each name card for the score, in a slightly different background colour and offset from the card underneath. Other ideas welcome.
